What I Do

I build internal developer platforms that let engineering teams move fast without breaking things. Instead of making developers wrestle with Kubernetes YAML, cloud permissions, deployment plumbing, or AI tooling sprawl, I create paved roads where teams push code and the platform handles the heavy lifting.

My edge is the combination of SRE discipline and practical AI engineering. I build AI operations copilots that summarize incidents from logs and traces, triage CI failures with context from Git history and deployment timelines, and draft runbook steps before a human engineer opens a terminal. I use model routing, prompt versioning, token budgets, and eval harnesses to keep AI features measurable, safe, and cost-controlled. The goal is not demos. The goal is fewer manual loops, faster incident recovery, and more reliable systems.

I have reduced cloud costs by 20-30% through FinOps practices, cut detection time with stronger observability, and automated away thousands of hours of manual infrastructure work. I work across AWS, Azure, Kubernetes, Terraform, GitOps, observability, compliance, and the emerging AI platform layer teams now need to operate safely.
